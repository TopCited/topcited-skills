Track your brand through **a repeatable set of customer questions**, then measure whether AI answers mention, recommend, or cite you. This is commonly called **AI visibility tracking**.

A practical setup:

1. **Choose 20–50 realistic prompts.** Include discovery (“best tools for [task]”), comparisons (“[your brand] vs [competitor]”), and buying questions (“best [category] for [customer type]”). Keep branded and unbranded questions separate.
2. **Run them regularly** across the platforms your customers use, such as ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity. Keep language, location, and search settings consistent; repeat runs because answers vary.
3. **Save the full answers and sources**, alongside these metrics:

| Metric | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Mention rate | Percentage of tested answers naming your brand |
| Recommendation rate | Percentage actively recommending you |
| Citation rate | Percentage linking to your website |
| Competitor presence | Which competing brands appear alongside or instead of you |
| Accuracy | Whether descriptions, pricing, and product claims are correct |

For tools, **Semrush Prompt Tracking** monitors a custom set of prompts, while **Ahrefs Brand Radar** offers broader brand and competitor visibility research. [Semrush documentation](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1503-prompt-tracking), [Ahrefs overview](https://ahrefs.com/academy/how-to-use-brand-radar/overview).

Also check **Bing Webmaster Tools’ AI Performance report** for citations of your pages across supported Microsoft AI experiences. [Bing documentation](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/ai-performance-9f8e7d6c).

**Start with a weekly spreadsheet before buying software.** Treat results as a sample of visibility—not a count of every real customer conversation. Track AI referral visits and conversions separately to see whether visibility produces business results.
