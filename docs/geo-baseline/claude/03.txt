There isn't one best AI visibility tracker. The right pick depends on your budget and needs. Most of the "best of" pages I found are written by vendors, so I wouldn't trust their rankings. The pricing figures below also come from those pages and conflict with each other, so confirm them on each vendor's site.

**By use case**
- **Small team or solo, on a budget:** Otterly looked like the cheapest dedicated tracker, with a Lite plan reported at about $29 a month.
- **Mid-market, dedicated tracking:** Peec AI turns up in nearly every roundup, with an entry price reported around €85 a month.
- **Enterprise:** Profound is the one most often named, though I couldn't pin down its price. One source lists $99 a month and another $499 a month.
- **You already use an SEO suite:** Semrush's AI toolkit, Ahrefs Brand Radar and SE Ranking (about $119 a month) are the add-on options. This is usually the cheapest way to start if you already pay for one of them.
- **Others that came up:** AthenaHQ (about $295 a month entry), Scrunch AI, Frase, Superlines and Topify.

**How to choose**
1. Check which AI engines it covers (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews, Claude). Coverage varies a lot.
2. Check how many prompts and how many brands or competitors the plan includes. This is usually where the price jumps.
3. Look for citation and source tracking, plus recommendations on what to fix. Tools that only report a visibility score are less useful.
4. Run two or three free trials on the same 20–30 prompts and compare the results. AI answers vary between runs, so one tool's numbers may not match another's.
