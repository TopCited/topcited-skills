# Stage 7 — Distribution

## Purpose (tell the user)
"Great content nobody links to stays invisible. We'll place your content
where your customers and the AI assistants actually look, which also earns
the links that build your site's authority."

## Primary TopCited operations
None — this is a route 2/3 stage. TopCited generated the content in Stage 6;
placing it is agent-direct and user-manual work.

## Flow
Work channel by channel, following the conversation contract for each.

**Routed action (agent-direct):** adapt the Stage 6 content per channel —
directory listings, community posts (Reddit/HN/forums), LinkedIn/X posts,
guest-post pitches — and draft the submission or outreach message. Anything
outward-facing (posting publicly, emailing anyone, submitting a third-party
form) requires explicit confirmation every single time, per the workflow's
route-2 guardrails — never post or submit on the user's behalf without that
confirmation, and never create third-party accounts.

**Routed action (user-manual):** the actual posting, from the user's own
accounts and directory logins. Give numbered instructions, add a checklist
item to `progress.md`, and follow up next session.
